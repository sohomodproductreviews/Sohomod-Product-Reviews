How to Use Sohomod Reviews Before Buying Furniture

Reading customer feedback is only one part of researching furniture. For a better purchasing decision, combine reviews with the individual product information provided by the retailer.

1. Check Furniture Dimensions

Measure the room where the furniture will be placed. Also consider doorways, elevators, staircases and hallways when purchasing larger pieces.

2. Review Materials and Construction

Look at the listed materials, upholstery, frame construction and other specifications. These details can help you determine whether a product suits your requirements.

3. Read Multiple Customer Reviews

One review represents one person's experience. Reading several reviews can give you a broader understanding of recurring positive and negative themes.

4. Consider Delivery Requirements

Furniture is often larger and heavier than ordinary online purchases. Check delivery information and make sure the receiving area is ready before ordering.

5. Compare Overall Value

Instead of looking only at price, consider quality, design, materials, dimensions, delivery and customer support when evaluating overall value.
